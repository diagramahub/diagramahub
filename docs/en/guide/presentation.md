# Presentation mode

Presentation mode hides the editor toolbar and the app sidebar so the diagram fills the window, ideal for reviewing it in a meeting or putting it on a projector.

## Entering and leaving

- Click **Present** in the editor's top bar.
- To leave, press ++esc++ or the close button (**Exit fullscreen (Esc)**) in the corner.

On entry the app sidebar hides automatically and comes back when you leave. A **Presentation Mode · Press Esc to exit** badge reminds you how to get back.

## Controls

During the presentation you get floating zoom controls:

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| **Zoom out** / **Zoom in** | Zooms the diagram out or in; the current percentage is shown between them |
| **Fit to screen** | Fits the whole diagram in the window |

You can also zoom with the mouse wheel and drag the diagram to move it.

!!! tip "Set up the view before presenting"
    Close the panels you don't need (code, description, chat) before entering. Zoom and position are saved with each diagram: frame the diagram the way you want to show it and it will open that way next time. For a clean on-screen background, choose the color and pattern under **Appearance** (see [The editor](editor.md#appearance)).

## Presenting without the editor

To show a diagram to people without an account, create a link with **Share diagram**: the shared view is read-only. See [Sharing diagrams](sharing.md).
