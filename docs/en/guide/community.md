# Community

The **Community** is the portal where users publish their diagrams so others can find them, star them, comment on them and **fork** them (an editable copy in their own account). Open it from **Community** in the sidebar; you need to be signed in.

## Browse

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Explore** | Every published diagram |
| **Featured** | The ones administrators pick |
| **My stars** | The ones you starred |
| **My publications** | Yours (also the ones hidden by moderation) |

You can **search** by title, author, tag or description, filter by **type** (MMD, PUML, D2, DBML) and **category** (Architecture, Databases, Business processes, DevOps, UML, Security, AI and data, Education), and **sort** by trending, stars, forks or date. *Trending* favors recent diagrams with more stars and forks.

## Publish a diagram

1. Open the diagram in the editor and use **Publish to the Community** (globe icon in the toolbar).
2. Choose the **category**, up to 5 **tags**, the **license** and the public description (the diagram's own by default).
3. Decide whether you allow **forks** and **comments**, and whether the diagram's changes are **published automatically**.
4. **Publish**.

| License | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **CC BY 4.0** | Use and modify, crediting the author |
| **CC0** | Public domain, no conditions |
| **CC BY-SA 4.0** | Like CC BY, sharing modifications under the same license |
| **All rights reserved** | View only: turns forks off |

The Community shows a **copy** of the code. Unless you turned on automatic publishing, use **Update with the current code** after changing the diagram. Each diagram can be published once. Whiteboard sketches can't be published yet.

!!! warning "Check the data before publishing"
    Every user of the instance can see a published diagram. Remove credentials, customer names or internal IPs before publishing it.

To **remove** it, use **Remove from the Community** in the editor or on its page: it stops being public and its stars and comments are deleted. Forks others already made are kept.

## Stars, forks and comments

- **Star**: mark the diagrams you like; find them under **My stars**.
- **Fork to my account**: copy the diagram into one of your projects to edit it. It counts for your plan's diagram limit, and the fork's description credits the author, the license and the link.
- **Comments**: public, in Markdown, with one level of replies. The diagram's author shows an **Author** badge. You can delete your comments; the diagram's author can delete any comment on their publication.

## Reports and moderation

Use **Report** on a diagram or comment that breaks the rules (spam, sensitive data, offensive content). With **3 reports** the content is hidden until an administrator reviews it.

Administrators see the **Reports** tab, and on each publication they can **feature** it, **hide** it, **restore** it (which closes its reports) or delete it.
